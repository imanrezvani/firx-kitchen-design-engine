Four Design Concepts

L-SHAPED + TALL PANTRY WALL

Concept A - The Ergonomic L

Style: Modern Minimalist Warm off-white lacquer + light oak floor, white quartz, brushed-steel accents

The sink is centered under the window for maximum natural light over the work zone. A clean L-shape opens toward the entrance, with the cooking wall on the north and a full-height fridge + pantry column closing the run. The compact work triangle (sink - hob - fridge = 5.3 m) keeps every move efficient while leaving the window wall completely free for daylight.

  • Sink centered under window, 900 base with undermount sink
  • Cooking wall: 60 cm oven under 4-zone induction hob + extractor
  • Full-height fridge column and 600 pantry with pull-outs
  • Upper cabinets to ceiling on both runs for dust-free storage
  • Over-window cabinet integrates the window wall cleanly
  • Work triangle 5.3 m - well inside the 7.9 m NKBA guideline
U-SHAPED - THREE WORKING WALLS

Concept B - The Storage U

Style: Warm Walnut / Craftsman American walnut cabinetry, stone-gray quartz, bronze hardware

Three full runs enclose the work zone for the maximum linear storage in the room. The west wall keeps the sink on the window, the north wall carries cooking plus the tall column, and the new south wall adds a second prep and storage run opposite the entry. The open end of the U faces the door, so the space still reads open and welcoming.

  • Longest linear storage: three runs totalling 8.4 m of cabinetry
  • Prep run opposite the cooking wall keeps two cooks working side by side
  • U mouth opens to the entrance - clear sightline from the door
  • Tall fridge + pantry on the far leg, away from the door swing
  • Generous 1.8 m aisle between runs
L-SHAPED + FREESTANDING ISLAND

Concept C - The L + Island

Style: Contemporary Two-Tone Graphite lowers, cream uppers, white quartz, oak island base

The L-shape stays identical to Concept A, but a freestanding island with a 300 mm overhang adds a breakfast bar for two, extra prep surface and under-island storage. Pendant lighting anchors the island over the seating zone. The island creates a natural circulation loop between entry, cooking zone and dining area.

  • Island 1500 x 900 with 300 mm overhang for two bar stools
  • Two pendants frame the seating zone
  • Prep surface right beside the cooking wall
  • Under-island drawer bank and open shelf for cookbooks
  • 900+ mm clearances maintained on all island sides
L-SHAPED WITH DINING ZONE

Concept D - The L + Dining Corner

Style: Scandinavian Light Pale oak lowers, cream uppers, white quartz, terracotta accents

A full-height buffet wall and a built-in banquette turn the east side of the room into a proper kitchen-diner. The kitchen functions as a two-zone room: a quiet cooking zone by the window and a coffee-and-breakfast zone with seating for four at the table. Ideal for families that live in the kitchen.

  • Dining table 1400 x 800 with banquette seating for four
  • Buffet / coffee station on the east wall (to ceiling)
  • Kitchen zone separated from dining zone by circulation
  • Breakfast preparation within arm's reach of the table

Selected Concept - The Ergonomic L (Concept A)

Why Concept A wins. It places the sink directly under the window so the most-used position gets the best daylight and outlook; it produces the shortest work triangle (5.3 m); it keeps the door and window completely unobstructed; it uses only simple L-run construction (two corners) for the cleanest build; and its open corner facing the entrance makes the 14 m² room feel larger. Concept B stores the most, but its extra corner and closed feel give the edge to A for ergonomics, light and construction simplicity.
